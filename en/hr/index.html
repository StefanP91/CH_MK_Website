Legal Aspects of Human Resources

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to independently:

  • To understand the obligations of the employee regarding the performance of work

  • To understand the obligations of the employer from the labor relationship

  • Providing the need for workers

  • Term and types of employment contract

  • Paying the job

  • Working hours

  • Breaks, vacations and absences from work

  • Special protection for certain categories of workers, worker associations and employers, and collective agreements

  • Termination of an Employment Agreement

  • Inspection supervision and court protection of labor rights

Performance Management

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to independently:

  • Implement a performance management system in a company

  • To create a detailed job description

  • To place kpi's

  • To set up a two -week KRA system

  • To issue an annual Performance Appraisal

  • To give feedback and summarize the results of the perfromens

Financial aspects of human resources

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to independently:

  • To identify in which organizational life cycle the company is and which are the HR activities related to that phase

  • To make financial planning and budgeting of human resources

  • To make a difference and put into operation financial and non -financial compensation

  • To calculate the HR impact of organizational growth

  • To use financial functions to get relevant company data

Employee Engagement

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to independently:

  • To identify the factors of Employee Engagement

  • Ways to Improve Employee Engagement

  • To measure employee satisfaction

  • To establish styles of communication and feedback

  • To make decisions and set goals

  • To determine employee motivation strategies

  • To create a questionnaire for measuring employee satisfaction, to summarize the results and to provide solutions

Training and development of employees

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to independently:

  • To create an employee training system from start using HR tools and software

  • To build a competence matrix and use the information to lay the groundwork for training and development

  • To understand, memorize and summarize the role of HR in the process of employee development and training

  • To set up a career road using HR tools and software

  • To put the training in the career path of an employee

Onboarding

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to independently:

  • To understand and create an onboarding process in a company from the beginning

  • To use software and tools for creating and monitoring the onboarding process

  • To maintain an UN Boothing process for new employees

  • To create an UN Boothing Presentation for New Employees

People Analytics

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to independently:

  • To recognize data-driven hr metics

  • To understand the process of people analytics and choose the appropriate method and analysis

  • To make an analysis of the workforce

  • To understand the database regulation

  • To build high -performance organizations based on metrics

  • To utilize metrics for employee engagement in order to improve the overall experience of employees

HR Business Partner

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to independently:

  • To identify the role of the HR business partner

  • To understand the hierarchical structure of the human resources department

  • To be able to set up a structure for an HR Business Partner department in a company on their own

HR strategy

Upon completion of the subject, students will be able to independently:

  • To create an annual strategy for a company of their choice and to include all the processes they worked on at the Academy

  • Learn how to create presentations and acquire presentation skills

  • Answer questions from management and defend the strategy they created
