Diploma

Based on the program chosen by students in Creative Hub, they can obtain a Creative Hub certificate, and an EU Diploma Supplement with ECTS credits.

Creative Hub Certificate
Issued to all students on all programs

  • This is an ideal choice for students who don't need a diploma and just want to acquire skills.
  • Our advice to students is to publish their Certificate on LinkedIn and add it to the education section, in order to highlight their skills.

EU Diploma Supplement with ECTS credits
Issued to students of EU certified programs and students of the Accelerated program

  • The EU Diploma Supplement with ECTS credits follows the model of the European Commission for non-formal education.
  • The Diploma Supplement contains detailed information about the student, the program, the total number of hours, and the ECTS credits earned by the student upon completion of the program.
  • We recommend students who plan to continue their education and career in the EU to choose a program that includes this diploma.
  • This EU Diploma is recognized by universities in Europe as additional credits when applying to the student's application.
