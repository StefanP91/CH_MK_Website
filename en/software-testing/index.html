Based on the program they choose, students at Creative Hub can obtain a Creative Hub certificate, and an EU Diploma Supplement with ECTS credits.

This is the ideal choice for students who do not need a diploma and just want to acquire skills.

EU Diploma Supplement with ECTS credits
This is issued to students of EU Certified programs and students of the Accelerated program.

  • The EU Diploma Supplement with ECTS credits follows the model of the European Commission for non-formal education.
  • The diploma supplement contains detailed information about the student, the program, the total number of hours, and the ECTS credits the student has acquired upon completion of the program.
  • We recommend that students who plan to continue their education and career in the EU choose a program that includes this diploma.
  • This EU Diploma Supplement is recognized by European universities as additional credits for the student's application.
